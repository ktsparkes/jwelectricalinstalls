What We Do

Safe, Reliable Electrical Work for Homes, Businesses and Farms

J.W Electrical Installations is an electrician based in Poole, covering Bournemouth, Christchurch and the wider Dorset area. We carry out domestic, commercial and industrial and agricultural electrical work, with a focus on safety, efficiency and practical solutions.

From additional sockets and lighting upgrades to full rewires and fault finding, no job is too big or too small. View all our services.

About Us

Meet James, Owner of J.W Electrical

James Watton founded J.W Electrical in 2022 with a commitment to delivering high-quality electrical services across Dorset. Though we're a small company, we've quickly built a reputation as a reliable and trusted provider of domestic, agricultural, industrial and commercial electrical work.

Customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. We take pride in a friendly approach, a strong work ethic and attention to detail, making sure every project is completed to a high standard. Read more about us.
