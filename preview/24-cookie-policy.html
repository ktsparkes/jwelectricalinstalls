Cookie Policy

How J.W Electrical Installations uses cookies on this website and how you can control them.

Last updated: October 2026

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work and can tell the owner how the site is being used.

02Cookies we use

  • Essential: your cookie choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key jw_cookie_consent) so we don't ask you every time.
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, set only if you click Accept. They help us understand which pages are useful.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

03Changing your choice

Click Cookie Settings in the footer of any page to change your choice at any time. If you reject analytics cookies, any existing Google Analytics cookies are removed.

04Browser controls

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may mean we ask for your cookie choice on each visit.

05Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
