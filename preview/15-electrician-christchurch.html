A Trusted Electrician for Christchurch

Christchurch sits just east of Bournemouth, an easy drive from our base in Poole. We cover the whole area, from the town centre and Purewell to Highcliffe, Mudeford and Burton.

Christchurch has a high number of bungalows and coastal homes, along with older cottages around the historic town centre. Bungalows are often well suited to rewiring and lighting upgrades thanks to loft access, while coastal properties need outdoor fittings that can cope with sea air. Do you cover Highcliffe and Mudeford?

Yes. We cover the whole Christchurch area, including Highcliffe, Mudeford, Friars Cliff, Somerford, Burton and Walkford.

Can you rewire a bungalow?

Yes. Bungalows are often well suited to rewiring because cables can usually be routed through the loft, which helps reduce disruption. We'll explain the best approach when we quote.

Do you install outdoor lighting on coastal homes?

Yes. We'll recommend outdoor fittings suited to coastal conditions so they last longer in salty sea air.
