Overview & Expertise

Finding the Fault, Not Just the Symptom

A circuit that keeps tripping, sockets that have stopped working or lights that flicker are all signs that something isn't right. The cause could be a faulty appliance, a damaged cable, a loose connection or water getting into a fitting.

We use a methodical approach and proper test equipment to trace the problem to its source. Once we've found it, we'll explain what's wrong and what it will take to put it right before carrying out the repair.

If testing shows your wiring is in poor condition overall, we'll tell you honestly and talk you through options such as a partial or full rewire.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cabling & Fault Finding FAQs

Why does my electricity keep tripping?

Tripping is usually your electrics protecting you from a fault. Common causes include a faulty appliance, a damaged cable, moisture in an outdoor fitting or an overloaded circuit. We can trace the cause and fix it safely.

Can you find intermittent faults?

Yes. Intermittent faults can take longer to track down, but methodical testing usually narrows down the cause. Telling us when the fault happens helps a lot.

Some of my sockets have stopped working. What should I do?

Check whether a switch has tripped in your consumer unit (fuse box). If it won't reset, or keeps tripping, stop using that circuit and get in touch.

Do you install new cabling?

Yes. We install new cable runs for extensions, garages, outbuildings, equipment and commercial and agricultural premises.
