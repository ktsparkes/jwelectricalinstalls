What We Do

Electrical Services Across Poole & Dorset

J.W Electrical Installations provides a full range of electrical services from our base in Poole. We look after homes, businesses and farms across Bournemouth, Christchurch, Broadstone, Wimborne, Ferndown and the wider Dorset area.

For homeowners, we install additional sockets and power points, interior and exterior lighting and electric heating, and carry out full and partial rewires.

For businesses, we provide commercial and industrial electrical work, including power, LED lighting and conduit and containment systems. For farms and rural properties, our agricultural electrical services cover barns, stables, yards and outbuildings.

And when something goes wrong, our cabling and fault finding service traces the problem and fixes it safely. Every job comes with a free, competitive quote.
