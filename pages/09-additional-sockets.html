Overview & Expertise

Power Where You Actually Need It

Most homes were wired for far fewer appliances than we use today. Overloaded extension leads and adaptors are a common cause of electrical problems, so properly installed sockets are a simple way to make your home safer and more convenient.

We install double sockets, USB charging sockets, weatherproof outdoor sockets, and power for home offices, kitchens, garages, garden rooms and workshops. Where possible, we run cables neatly and out of sight.

Before adding new sockets we check that the existing circuit can safely take the extra load. If it can't, we'll explain your options, such as a new dedicated circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Additional Sockets & Power Points FAQs

Can you add sockets without chasing walls?

Often, yes. Depending on your walls and floors, we can sometimes run cables under floors or through voids. Where chasing is needed, we keep it neat.

Can you fit USB sockets?

Yes. USB and USB-C charging sockets can replace standard sockets or be added as new ones.

Can you install an outdoor socket?

Yes. We install weatherproof outdoor sockets for garden tools, pressure washers, lighting and outdoor living.

Is it safe to keep using extension leads?

Occasional use is fine, but relying on extension leads and adaptors long term can overload sockets. Adding properly installed sockets is the safer option.
