02What information we collect

We only collect the information we need to respond to you and carry out your work:

  • Enquiry details such as your name, email address, phone number, postcode and message when you use our contact form, email or call us.
  • Job information such as the address of the property and details or photos of the work needed.
  • Website usage data such as pages visited and device type, collected through Google Analytics only if you accept analytics cookies.

03How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide a quote.
  • To arrange, carry out and invoice for work.
  • To keep records of work carried out.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it (analytics, with your consent).

We will never sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

04Our legal basis

  • Contract: to quote for and carry out work you have asked for.
  • Legitimate interests: to reply to enquiries and run our business.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial and tax records.
  • Consent: for optional analytics cookies, which you can withdraw at any time.

05Cookies and Google Analytics

We use essential cookies to make the site work and, only if you accept, Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. See our Cookie Policy for details. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer, or opt out of Google Analytics entirely at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

06Who we share it with

We only share your information where necessary, for example with:

  • our website host and form provider (Hostinger and Web3Forms), who process data on our behalf;
  • Google, for website analytics (only with your consent);
  • our accountant and HMRC, for tax and accounting purposes;
  • suppliers or other trades, only where needed to complete your job and with your knowledge.

We never sell your personal information.

07How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 12 months.
  • Customer and job records: up to 6 years, or longer if needed for warranty purposes.
  • Financial records: as long as the law requires.

08Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw consent and to data portability. Email us and we'll respond within one month.

09Keeping your data secure

We take sensible steps to protect your information, including secure, password-protected accounts and only keeping what we need.

10Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
